How it is formed

Active and Passive Voice

Active voice: the subject performs the action. Passive voice: the subject receives the action.

Active: Subject + Verb + Object
Passive: Object + suitable form of “be” + past participle (V3) + (by + doer)
Active
Passive

Note: Passive voice is normally formed from transitive verbs (verbs that take an object). Some continuous and perfect forms are uncommon in everyday use, but their structures are useful for grammar study.
